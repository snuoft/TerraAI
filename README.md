# Introduction
In our fast growing world, climate change has become an increasingly dangerous threat to our whole world. According to a reasearch by NASA(National Aeronautics and Space Administration), climate change is driving more 'volatile precipitation patterns' around the world. Not only can this can lead to a longer monsoon season every year, but it can also increase the potential risks of landslides in such areas. With the very recent [Wayand tragedy](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cd108y8439po) in the state of Kerala, India, the urge for the need of a well developed technology that can monitor real-time landslide triggers around the world, has become crucial.  
Landslides become a critical hazard when:  
1. Local communities lack access to reliable weather warnings.  
2. Residents are unfamiliar with the area's geographical risks, especially newcomers.  
3. There is limited awareness of the region’s history of landslide occurrences and trends.

# Current Solutions  
1. LEWS(Landslide Early warning systems) : These systems monitor rainfall, soil moisture, and ground movement to predict potential landslides but often limited to specific geographical areas and can have high false alarm rates.
2. Satellite Remote Sensing : Predicts landslides based on terrain changes and weather patterns. However, satellite data can be costly and may have time delays in data availability.
3. Drone-Based Monitoring : Drones equipped with sensors collect high-resolution imagery and data to monitor slopes and terrain. But they require skilled operators for effective data collection and analysis.


# TerraAI
Introducing TerraAI, where safeguarding communities from landslide risks becomes a reality. Powered by NVIDIA AI Workbench and advanced geospatial analytics, TerraAI provides users with real-time insights and alerts, making safety accessible and fostering resilience in vulnerable areas.  
**NVIDIA AI Workbench** helps TerraAI by speeding up AI model training, improving performance, and providing scalable deployment for accurate disaster predictions in real-time. It enhances the speed, scalability, and accuracy of TerraAI's, helping the platform achieve its goal of empowering communities with reliable, real-time insights to better prepare for and respond to natural disasters.   
TerraAI uses **Python 2.1 with CUDA 12.2** that allows the AI models to run more efficiently by using the parallel processing power of NVIDIA GPUs.
